    Your first brew day

    Six steps from tin to tinnie

    A standard 23 litre beer kit, start to finish. Easier than assembling flat pack furniture, and the reward is better.

    1. Clean and sanitise

      Everything that touches the brew gets sanitised. This one step decides most of how your beer turns out.

      10 min

    2. Mix the wort

      Dissolve the kit and brew enhancer in hot water, then top up the fermenter with cool water to 23 L.

      20 min

    3. Pitch the yeast

      Check the temperature, take your starting gravity with the hydrometer, sprinkle the yeast and seal the lid.

      OG reading

    4. Ferment

      Keep it steady and out of the Bundy heat. The airlock bubbles, then slows. Same gravity two days running means it's done.

      7 to 10 days · 18 to 22°C

    5. Bottle and prime

      Add carbonation drops or priming sugar to each bottle, fill, and cap. Our capper makes this oddly satisfying.

      FG reading

    6. Condition, then crack one

      Leave the bottles somewhere warm to carbonate, then move them to the fridge. Patience pays here.

      2+ weeks

    Brewer's maths

    How strong is my brew?

    Pop in your hydrometer readings. Original gravity (OG) is taken before you pitch the yeast; final gravity (FG) when fermentation finishes.

    Estimate only, using the standard (OG − FG) × 131.25 formula. Standard drinks use the Australian measure of 10 g of alcohol.

    5.0%ABV
